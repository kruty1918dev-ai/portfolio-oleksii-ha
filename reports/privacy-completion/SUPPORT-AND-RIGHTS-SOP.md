# Owner-managed correspondence and data-rights procedure

Prepared procedure; mailbox-side implementation/backup handling remains to be
confirmed. It does not authorize the assistant to read/send/delete mailbox data.

- Monitor privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de and support@oleksiy-havryliuk.de. Record only
  the request ID, received date, necessary context, deadline, action and closure.
- For statutory rights, normally answer within one month. If a lawful extension
  is necessary, explain it and its reason within the initial period.
- Identify which record is involved: a local game save, mailbox conversation,
  Analytics record, Crashlytics report, ad-provider data or hosting request. An
  email address alone may not locate pseudonymous Google records.
- Use proportionate verification. Do not request a passport by default. Do not
  create a new permanent cross-provider user ID just to support requests.
- Local erase removes owned save/recovery/document-cache files. It does not
  automatically erase Android backups, provider records or every SDK preference.
- For Analytics/Crashlytics requests follow the actual available provider process
  for the supplied identifier/context. ResetAnalyticsData is not server deletion.
  Escalate to the applicable provider where needed; explain identification limits
  and lawful exceptions instead of promising impossible universal deletion.
- At case closure, set a deletion deadline 12 calendar months later. Remove the
  closed correspondence and unnecessary attachments by that deadline unless a
  specific legal duty or necessary legal-claims retention applies. Document any
  exception and its next review date. A permanent archive is not deletion.
- Confirm how the mail provider handles trash, retained copies and backups.
  Do not claim deletion from systems that the owner cannot control or verify.
- Keep credentials private, use account access controls and secure transport;
  share only the information necessary to resolve a request.
