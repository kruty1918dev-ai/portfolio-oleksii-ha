2. Scope and your choices

This policy review covers Quiet Camp for Android (com.quietcamp.game), local saves, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and AdMob rewarded advertising. Firebase Core, Analytics and Crashlytics 14.0.0 and Google Mobile Ads 11.6.0 are present in the audited source. No player account, authentication, cloud saves, active purchase service, Remote Config, working banner/interstitial implementation or additional mediation SDK was identified in that source. New services require a fresh assessment and policy update.

Reading or acknowledging the policy is not consent. Age categories are unknown, under 13, 13–15, 16–17 and 18+, without birth date or documents. Unknown age and under 16 disable optional Analytics, crash uploads and ad/UMP requests through the game. Ages 16+ need three independent, initially off choices; refusal leaves the local game and documents available. Age changes revoke optional permissions. Production gates are closed; publisher ad-unit IDs are empty.

C# Firebase initialization waits for an eligible, saved Analytics or crash opt-in. The game’s Android startup provider calls no Firebase API; the source manifest excludes FirebaseInitProvider. Shared Core can create installed native products for a single purpose. Persisted overrides, identifiers, files, automatic collection and traffic differ from game-side checks: absence of early storage/traffic is unproven. Mobile Ads’ startup provider and delayed-measurement flag also require native verification.

Quiet Camp is offline-first, not purely offline. Enabled services contact Google; opening this page contacts its host. Explicit Google sample ads are development-only and need the same age gate and separate local advertising choice. Sample app/units lack a publisher UMP message and skip that production form flow. Test ads still process technical data; they do not prove a production consent configuration.

4. Local progress and privacy settings

The game stores arrangements, progress, album, tutorials, language, accessibility/audio/display settings, local balances, document acknowledgements, age category and separate optional choices with versions/revisions. A local installation marker is never transmitted as an identifier. Android keeps it in noBackupFilesDir; a restored save without a matching marker, an invalid receipt or recovery from a save backup requires new choices. A failed privacy save leaves services off. Recovery copies and bundled/cached documents can remain locally. The save system does not upload these records.

Where these records are personal data, necessary game storage is assessed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where a valid contractual basis exists and §25(2) TDDDG for strictly necessary terminal storage. This exemption does not authorize optional tracking. Minimal local choice/version receipts support applying and demonstrating decisions under Article 6(1)(c) where accountability duties require them. Age category controls options; it does not verify identity.

Local data remains until removed using game/device controls or app removal, subject to Android, manufacturer backup and device-transfer behavior. The final release backup rules must be verified. Local erasure or uninstallation does not automatically remove platform backups, SDK records or data already held by Google.

5. Optional Firebase Analytics

Optional gameplay analysis helps identify difficult levels, assess controls/accessibility and investigate performance problems. The current code emits ten custom event names: qc_level_begin, qc_level_summary, level_fail, qc_screen, qc_comfort_setting, qc_comfort_feedback, qc_frame_budget, rewarded_ad_requested, rewarded_ad_completed and reward_granted. They describe level starts and outcomes, a failed puzzle check, grouped screen/settings/feedback choices, performance bands and the requested/completed/granted stages of an ad reward.

Parameters are numeric categories, level/grid/preset/schema information, grouped duration, bounded action/rule-failure counts, three fixed voluntary feedback choices and frame-time/quality bands. Reward category distinguishes an extra life from a cosmetic reward. Reward completion follows the SDK reward-and-close result; reward_granted is emitted only after a successful save. The base campaign has 100 levels; the event validator accepts indices up to 10000 for content compatibility, not proof of 10000 active levels. Custom events contain no player name, free-text feedback or raw gesture trace. The local custom-event limit of 120 per Analytics service instance is not a cap on SDK automatic collection.

Firebase Analytics can also process app-instance identifiers, device/OS and app information, language and automatically collected events such as first open, sessions and engagement, depending on SDK configuration. Network requests use an IP address and can support approximate geographic derivation without a precise-location permission. No player account does not mean anonymous data.

Optional Analytics needs age 16+ and a separate saved current opt-in under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and §25(1) TDDDG where applicable. Analytics storage is granted only for that choice; ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization stay denied. Android source defaults are off/denied; no custom player ID is set. Crash consent does not authorize Analytics. After shared Core creation, collection is explicitly denied before either adapter enables its own purpose. Native startup, automatic events and old persistent overrides remain release checks.

Use Settings → Data & documents → Help improve the game to change the choice. Withdrawal disables further collection through the adapter and invokes a local Analytics reset, including the app-instance identifier. The exact native effect must be verified on Android. Reset does not erase data previously uploaded; contact the privacy address for an applicable server-data request.

6. Optional Firebase Crashlytics

Optional Crashlytics reports help reproduce failures and repair stability defects. SDK diagnostics can include stack traces, exception messages, timestamps, device model, OS, app version, memory/storage information and crash/session/installation identifiers. Analytics breadcrumbs may accompany reports when Analytics is also enabled. SDK-generated logs and real exception messages can contain incidental information; no blanket guarantee of anonymous or fully sanitized reports is made.

The configured custom keys are game_version, game_mode (campaign), level_index and failure_area. The game submits fixed diagnostic categories, including save failures, rather than copying save contents, file paths or free-text player input into its manual diagnostic message. Reports for the same category are locally deduplicated. This does not sanitize every diagnostic that the installed SDK may capture. No custom player user ID is configured.

Crash reporting needs age 16+ and independent consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and §25(1) TDDDG where applicable. Analytics does not authorize crash upload. Manifest defaults and runtime collection controls are off; the adapter never persists auto-upload as enabled. The game calls Unity Crashlytics APIs through its authorized crash adapter; SDK-internal handler behavior is not yet verified. Shared Core can create native Crashlytics components in Analytics-only operation. Native local capture, persistent overrides and early transmission remain unverified release blockers.

Pending reports are sent only when the previous-session receipt and present crash choice both permit it. First opt-in during a session discards older pending reports. Withdrawal requests deletion through an existing initialized transport; an SDK never started by the game is not started just for cleanup. Game-side requests are issued in order; stale game-side completion cannot re-enable reporting. Failure to issue a request leaves the transport disabled until restart. Android Crashlytics 21.0.0 send/delete APIs return void: their completion or actual deletion cannot be inferred from the call. Native deletion, files, payloads and transmission already in progress need device testing; withdrawal cannot recall an upload already sent.

Use Settings → Data & documents → Crash reports to change the independent choice. Withdrawal prevents further reporting through the adapter and requests cleanup of pending reports; it does not remove reports already uploaded. Requests concerning uploaded records go to the privacy contact. The diagnostic test button is restricted to Editor/development versions and requires the reporting choice; no automatic fatal test is run.

7. Rewarded advertising and privacy options

AdMob supplies optional rewarded videos. A completed SDK reward callback followed by ad closure can grant an extra life or a cosmetic lantern. Extra-life rewards are limited to ten per UTC day; this is not a universal cap on cosmetic rewards. Failed or skipped ads do not grant a reward. The lantern can also be earned through ordinary play. No working banner, interstitial or extra mediation adapter was identified.

AdMob can process IP address and derived approximate location, ad interactions such as taps/video views, technical performance data, advertising ID, app-set ID and other permitted SDK identifiers. Purposes include delivering a requested ad, measuring advertising performance and preventing fraud. Non-personalized ads also process personal data and can require consent for device storage/access.

For publisher units, eligible age 16+ and a separate saved ad choice precede any game-side UMP request. Information is refreshed for an authorized launch and explicit video request; required forms and CanRequestAds precede Mobile Ads initialization/loading. For EEA/UK/Switzerland, Android additionally checks fresh UMP information and device-storage purpose/Google-vendor consent signals. Absent/denied signals block loading rather than falling back to Limited Ads. Actual message coverage and selected vendors still require console and native verification.

Every rewarded request sets npa=1. Ages 16–17 use Google’s AgeRestrictedTreatment.Teen; adults use Unspecified, with a T ad-content ceiling. Unknown age and under 16 cannot request ads or UMP through the game. CanRequestAds alone is not all-purpose consent: non-personalized ads still need applicable device-access/vendor permissions. These controls do not certify actual console configuration or wire payload.

The selected basis for relevant optional advertising processing/device access is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and §25(1) TDDDG where applicable, using valid purpose/vendor choices. Provider fraud/security purposes need their own applicable grounds. No general legitimate-interest justification or valid personalized-ad permission is inferred from the current implementation. Legal bases and vendor choices must be resolved for the actual release.

You can refuse advertising or disable it in Settings → Data & documents → Optional advertising. If Google requires privacy options after a prior authorized SDK interaction, its form remains available to eligible users through Data & documents. Show optional video bonuses is a separate availability preference, not consent. Withdrawal blocks new requests and invalidates a pending reward; a visible ad/form keeps the game gate until closure. This does not prove all background SDK connections stop or erase stored UMP choices. This webpage cannot manage game consent. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

8. Children and teenagers

Quiet Camp is intended for ages 13+. A neutral age-category selector asks neither birth date nor documents. Under 13 explains the intended minimum without claiming identity verification or parental permission. Ages 13–15 can play locally. Unknown age and under 16 disable the game’s optional Analytics, crash uploads and ad/UMP requests.

Ages 16–17 and adults can independently opt into Analytics, crash reports and advertising; choices start off. All eligible ads are non-personalized, with teen treatment for 16–17. Age changes revoke choices; stale/corrupt receipts and restored backups grant no permission. Native behavior needs verification before release. There is no parental-consent workflow.

Article 8 GDPR applies to consent-based information-society services offered directly to children. Germany generally uses 16 as the threshold; national implementations differ. The actual target audience, Play Console age groups, content and marketing must agree. A 13+ declaration does not by itself exclude Families requirements or other protections. The underage consent and advertising review is a release blocker.

11. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until removed, subject to the backup limitations described above. The release profile selects two months for user/event-level data governed by the GA4 retention setting, without unnecessary renewal through new activity. This must be confirmed in the actual property. It is not a universal expiry for standard aggregated reports, identifiers or separately exported data; no unnecessary export is included in this profile.

Google states that Crashlytics retains crash traces, extracted minidump data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting their removal from live and backup systems. This does not promise complete deletion on day 90. Raw NDK minidumps are stored temporarily for processing. Advertising retention follows the applicable Google and selected-provider terms; no unsupported game-specific deletion period is promised.

Correspondence uses the 12-month schedule and lawful exceptions above. Actual hosting-log and backup retention must be established from the hosting arrangement. Different providers, aggregated reports and local copies do not share one universal retention clock.

To request access or deletion, email privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de with the app version, approximate dates and relevant context. An email address alone may not identify pseudonymous SDK records. Proportionate verification may be needed; do not send an identity document by default. We will explain identification limits, provider procedures and lawful exceptions. Local reset, consent withdrawal and advertising-ID reset do not themselves delete already uploaded records.

12. Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation; objection to direct marketing is available at any time. Portability applies to qualifying automated processing based on consent or contract. These rights have conditions and statutory exceptions.

You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing. Contact privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de. We normally respond within one month; legally permitted extensions and reasons will be communicated. You can complain to a supervisory authority, particularly where you live, work or believe a breach occurred. The German data protection authority directory helps locate an appropriate authority. Reading or acknowledging this policy is not consent.

The Vercel host receives connection/request information, such as IP address, path, time and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. The basis for proportionate necessary delivery/security processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Host contracts, log settings, retention and transfer safeguards require account-specific confirmation.

Read the separate website privacy notice for hosting and correspondence, and the legal notice for the operator. The illustrated guide is separately hosted on GitHub Pages. Following an external link connects your browser to that provider.

Reasonable safeguards include HTTPS delivery, restrictive website content-security settings, minimal optional data and validated local saves with recovery copies. Recovery copies protect integrity and are not a claim of encryption. No system guarantees absolute security. The actual Android release and account controls must be verified independently of this webpage.
