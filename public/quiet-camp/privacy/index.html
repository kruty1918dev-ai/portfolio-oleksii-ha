1. Who is responsible

Quiet Camp is an offline-first spatial puzzle game with a base campaign of 100 levels in 10 chapters. Its developer and data controller is the natural person Олексій Гаврилюк Олександрович, based in Germany. Oleksiy Havryliuk is the English name supplied for correspondence; its spelling has not yet been checked against an official identity document. Kruty1918 Games is a brand and trading name, not a separate registered company.

Contact the controller at privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de. A legally suitable postal address is still being resolved. This omission must be addressed before the document or legal notice is represented as ready for release.

02

2. What has been verified

The committed project was audited on 20 October 2026. Core gameplay and saves are local. No active account, authentication, cloud-save, remote-configuration or purchase service was found in the audited game configuration. Game characters and guests are fictional game content, not contacts from your device.

Google Mobile Ads Unity 11.6.0 is declared. The current configuration uses Google sample rewarded ads in development environments; production ads require separate publication checks. Firebase Analytics collection is configured off and its adapter is conditional. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics were reported as installed in a newer local project, but that installation, its settings and network behavior were not available for verification. Descriptions of those services below are conditional, not confirmation that they run in a released version.

The game is not described as purely offline: advertising and any activated analytics or crash SDK can connect to external servers. Visiting this policy also involves a connection to the website host.

4. Local progress and settings

The game stores the current puzzle and tent placements, completed levels, camp album, tutorials, language, sound, display and accessibility settings, local resource balances and privacy-choice receipts. Save recovery copies and a cached legal document may also be stored. These records are not uploaded by the audited save system.

Local storage supports resuming play and retaining your choices. Where these records are personal data, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, to provide the game functions you request. Storage strictly necessary for those functions is assessed separately from optional tracking under German terminal-equipment rules (§25 TDDDG).

Local data lasts until you erase it or remove the app, subject to Android or manufacturer backup and device-transfer behavior. The repository does not establish the final Android Auto Backup rules. Uninstalling or erasing a local save does not necessarily remove platform backups, SDK storage or server records. Use the game’s local-data controls and Android app-storage and backup settings as appropriate.

05

5. Optional Firebase Analytics

The audited adapter supports a separate opt-in for gameplay analysis. Its purposes are to find difficult levels, assess controls and accessibility choices, and detect performance problems. Implemented custom categories cover level starts and summaries, counts of actions and rule failures, duration bands, screen categories, grouped settings, three fixed feedback choices and frame-time bands. Parameters are numeric; the adapter does not submit free-text feedback or raw gesture traces. Level measurements currently accept levels 1–30, despite the 100-level campaign.

The previously proposed events such as game_started, level_complete and rewarded_ad_completed were not found as emitted events. They must not be treated as collected data. If Firebase Analytics is enabled in a release, its own collection can additionally include app-instance identifiers, device and OS information, app version, language, session or engagement events and other automatically collected events. IP addresses are processed for network communication and may be used for coarse geographic derivation; no device location permission was found. No account does not mean anonymous analytics.

The intended basis for optional gameplay analytics is consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where required, §25(1) TDDDG. In the committed configuration analytics is disabled. The conditional adapter grants analytics storage only after an opt-in and denies ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization. These code settings do not prove the configuration of a newer installed SDK or linked Google accounts.

Where available, change the analytics choice under Settings → Privacy → Analytics. Withdrawal stops collection through this adapter and resets local Analytics data and the app-instance ID; it does not itself delete information already sent to Google. Use the privacy email for a server-data request. Declining analytics does not prevent core play.

6. Firebase Crashlytics diagnostics

Crashlytics installation has been reported, but no Crashlytics SDK, initialization, collection override, custom key, user ID or diagnostic-log call was found in the audited committed project. We cannot verify a release-specific opt-out or claim that crash reporting is off by default. Google’s standard Crashlytics behavior is automatic crash-report collection when integrated, unless configured otherwise.

When active, Crashlytics can process stack traces and exception messages, timestamps, app version, device model, OS, memory and storage information, crash/session and installation identifiers, and configured logs or keys. Analytics breadcrumbs can be attached when Analytics is integrated and its collection allows them. The purpose is to reproduce failures and repair stability problems. Diagnostic messages must be checked for accidental personal information.

The actual collection controls and lawful basis must be fixed before release. A narrowly limited stability-reporting setup might rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR only after a documented necessity and interests assessment and separate assessment of terminal access; an optional consent-based setup would require Article 6(1)(a) and an effective choice. Neither assessment nor a consent implementation has been verified here. The Analytics switch must not be presented as a Crashlytics switch.

7. Advertising and privacy choices

The implemented format is a user-requested rewarded video for an extra life. The game grants the local reward only after the SDK reports it earned and the ad closes; failed or skipped videos do not grant it. The local economy limits these rewards to ten per UTC day. No working banner, interstitial or additional mediation adapter was found. Production AdMob messages and selected advertising partners cannot be established from the repository.

When ads are requested, Google Mobile Ads may transmit the IP address (which may indicate approximate location), ad interactions such as taps and video views, technical performance data, Android advertising ID, app-set ID and other SDK identifiers where permitted. Advertising and measurement are not made anonymous by selecting non-personalized ads. Device and SDK settings affect which identifiers are available.

For publisher inventory, the code requests updated UMP consent information, displays a form if required and checks whether ads can be requested before initializing and loading an ad. It does so on an explicit reward request, not at each launch. Google recommends updating consent information each launch; this difference needs review. Google sample inventory in development bypasses publisher messaging. No separate personalized/non-personalized request mode or underage flag is set by the game code; production behavior depends on valid choices, account configuration and SDK behavior.

Where legally required, storage/access for advertising and personalized advertising need consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and §25(1) TDDDG. Consent and vendor-specific purposes must be shown in the actual in-app message. Fraud-prevention or other provider purposes need their own valid basis; no blanket legitimate-interest justification is asserted here. A production vendor list and the applicable terms must be verified before ads are enabled.

When UMP reports that privacy options are required, the game exposes an advertising privacy-options action in Settings → Privacy to reopen the Google form. This is separate from the analytics switch. Android advertising-ID controls are in device settings. This webpage has no consent toggle and cannot change in-app choices. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information. The final in-app form must let users inspect the selected vendors.

8. Children and teenagers

Quiet Camp is intended for ages 13+. That intention is not age verification or permission to track every teenager. The audited implementation has a minimum-age setting but no verified age screen, parental-authorization process or underage advertising treatment.

For an information-society service offered directly to a child on the basis of consent, Article 8 GDPR applies. In Germany, a person below 16 generally needs authorization from a holder of parental responsibility for that consent; thresholds differ by country. This rule does not automatically make all game processing consent-based. Optional analytics and advertising must account for the relevant age and country, with tracking withheld where valid consent cannot be established.

Before release, the developer must resolve the handling of users aged 13–15 and 16–17, set applicable ad restrictions in both UMP and ad requests, and align the Play Console target-audience declaration with the actual audience. UMP does not automatically pass its under-age-of-consent flag to Mobile Ads. Google Play Families rules must be considered wherever children are included in the applicable audience. No parental consent, age checks or teen-safe personalized advertising are claimed as implemented. Please contact the privacy email if you believe a child’s data was processed improperly.

9. Support and privacy correspondence

If you email us, we process your address, message, attachments and correspondence metadata to answer the request. Please send only the information necessary to describe the problem; avoid sending sensitive data or complete device backups.

Game support necessary to fulfil a user’s contractual request can fall under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other ordinary correspondence relies on Article 6(1)(f), the interest in answering enquiries, subject to the sender’s rights. Handling statutory privacy requests relies on Article 6(1)(c). A specific lawful retention duty may require limited records to be retained. These bases do not authorize optional SDK tracking.

Messages are handled by the developer and the configured mail provider. Domain registration with STRATO does not prove who hosts the mailboxes. The mail provider, processing terms and mailbox operation still need confirmation. The intended deletion rule is 12 months after case closure, unless a specific legal duty or necessary legal-claims retention applies; operational implementation of that schedule has not yet been verified.

Google commonly acts as processor for Crashlytics customer data; Analytics has separate terms, and advertising roles depend on the relevant service and purpose. The exact account contracting entity, vendor selection, data-sharing settings, linked products and accepted agreements are not accessible in this audit. They require confirmation before publication.

Google services may process information outside the EEA, including in the United States. Applicable safeguards can include an adequacy decision for a certified recipient under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses under the relevant agreement, with supplementary measures where needed. Their applicability must be checked for the actual recipients; no EEA-only storage is promised. You can request information about the applicable safeguards via the privacy contact.

Refer to Firebase privacy information, the Firebase processing terms, Google Ads processing terms and Google’s controller terms. Applicable recipient details and transfer arrangements must match the service actually enabled.

11. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on the device until removed, with the backup qualifications in section 4. The intended Analytics retention is two months for user/event-level data covered by the GA4 retention control. No console evidence confirms this setting. It does not set a universal lifetime for standard aggregated reports, separately exported data or all identifiers; reset-on-new-activity and linked exports must also be checked.

Google documents that Crashlytics retains crash traces, extracted minidump data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. This is a provider rule, not a promise of complete deletion on day 90. Raw NDK minidumps are held temporarily for processing. No custom retention override or local release configuration has been verified.

Advertising retention depends on the applicable Google and selected-vendor terms; no game-specific fixed deletion period has been established. Support correspondence has the intended 12-month rule described in section 9. Hosting-log retention must be confirmed with the actual hosting configuration.

Request deletion via privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de. Give the app version, approximate dates and relevant context. We may need proportionate verification and may be unable to identify pseudonymous SDK records from an email address alone. Do not send a passport by default. We will explain identification limits, applicable exceptions and available provider procedures. Local reset, advertising-ID reset and withdrawal do not by themselves delete previously uploaded records.

12. Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation; objection to direct marketing is available at any time. Portability applies to qualifying automated processing based on consent or contract. These rights have conditions and statutory exceptions.

You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing. Contact privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de. We normally respond within one month; legally permitted extensions and reasons will be communicated. You can complain to a supervisory authority, particularly where you live, work or believe a breach occurred. The German data protection authority directory helps locate an appropriate authority. Reading or acknowledging this policy is not consent.

13. This website and security

This legal page contains no analytics scripts, ad SDKs, third-party fonts, consent cookies or embedded media. Its essential text and language navigation work without JavaScript. The host still receives connection information such as IP address, request path, time and browser information to deliver the page and protect infrastructure. Delivery and proportionate security logging are assessed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the interest in providing a reliable, secure public document.

The current custom domain responds through Vercel; final hosting account, log controls, retention, data-processing agreement and transfer safeguards require confirmation before publication. Domain registration is with STRATO. The separate illustrated game website uses GitHub Pages and has its own request handling; following an external link connects you to that provider. Existing portfolio pages may use different assets and require a separate website privacy assessment.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the processing. The audited save system uses validation, atomic writes and recovery copies; these protect integrity and are not a claim of save-file encryption. Google documents encryption in transit for its relevant services. No system can guarantee absolute security. The actual release and hosting controls still need operational verification.
