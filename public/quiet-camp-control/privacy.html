5. Retention and deletion

Connection tokens are retained while the connection is active or until disconnected. Disconnecting through an approved service operation deletes that connection's stored tokens; revoke the platform grant separately in the provider's connected-app settings. App credentials are separately stored and must be replaced or removed separately.

Drafts, publication payloads, media, metrics snapshots, job records and audit history do not currently have a universal automatic deletion period. They remain stored until manually removed or handled through a deletion request; deleting a draft or disconnecting an account does not automatically erase all other records. We assess retention according to the continuing service purpose, security/error reconciliation and any applicable legal obligation, and remove data when it is no longer necessary. Hosting logs and backups follow the relevant provider's retention settings; immediate removal from backups is not promised.

For deletion or an export, contact privacy@oleksiy-havryliuk.de with the account or connection identifier, never a password or token. We verify identity proportionately. Tell us whether you want connection removal, service-record deletion or deletion of published platform content: these are different actions. Disconnecting does not delete videos already published to TikTok or YouTube.
